# BAM Maintenance — Public-Sector and Contract Roadmap

BAM Maintenance is an operating division of BAM Development. All registrations, insurances, policies, financial information and contracts remain under BAM Development.

## Initial service scope
- Responsive repairs
- Planned maintenance
- Voids and reinstatement
- Building-fabric works
- Compliance-led remedials
- Minor refurbishment and projects
- Regional subcontract delivery for established FM and housing contractors

## Bid-readiness pack
Company information, insurances, H&S, quality, environment, equality, modern slavery, business continuity, cyber security, safeguarding, resident care, complaints, subcontractor controls, social value, carbon reduction, case studies, references, rates and response standards.

## Route to market
1. Central supplier registration and tender alerts.
2. Relevant facilities-management and housing-maintenance DPS routes.
3. Local authority and housing-association minor works.
4. Regional delivery partnerships with national framework contractors.
5. Direct recurring contracts with hotels, managing agents and care operators.

## Commercial discipline
Bid only where geography, mobilisation cash, insurance, competence, response obligations and evidence are within BAM Development's actual capacity.
