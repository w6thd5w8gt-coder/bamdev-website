Important website terms.

The website provides general information about BAM Development’s intended services and operating model.

No appointment or duty to act

Submitting information, using the project intake, booking a call or receiving an acknowledgement does not create a professional appointment, contractual relationship, duty of care or obligation to act.

No project-specific advice

Website content and demonstration dashboards are not project-specific professional, legal, financial, fire, structural, building-control or other regulated advice.

Specialist appointments

BAM Development does not replace statutory designers, regulated professionals or specialist consultants where their appointment is required. Any BAM appointment will define scope, exclusions, dependencies and required specialist interfaces.

Evidence and claims

Public case material is anonymised and avoids unsupported client identities, values, outcomes, savings or completion claims.

Availability and accuracy

The website may be updated as BAM Development’s systems and services develop. A formal proposal or appointment takes precedence over website wording.
