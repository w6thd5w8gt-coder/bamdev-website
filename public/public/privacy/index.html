Information handling

Privacy information.

This page explains the current public website’s limited information handling. It is not a substitute for project-specific confidentiality or data-processing terms.

Current website operation

The public website is a static Cloudflare-hosted site. The project intake currently prepares an email on the visitor’s device. It does not upload project files to BAM Development’s website.

Information you choose to send

When you email BAM Development, the information is handled through the relevant email service and then reviewed to determine whether BAM Development can assist. Do not send unnecessary special-category personal data, passwords or security credentials.

Analytics

The codebase includes privacy-conscious event hooks for future measurement. No third-party analytics provider is activated in the release package unless separately configured.

Project confidentiality

Project-specific confidentiality, professional appointments, data-processing requirements and secure document routes will be confirmed separately before substantive work begins.

Contact

Questions about website information handling may be sent to bmirza@bamdevelopment.co.uk.
