One operating model

Four routes into owner-side project control.

The right route depends on the decision, exposure and stage of the project. Every appointment remains under BAM Development.

01 / DIAGNOSTIC

Establish the position.

For acquisition, feasibility, cost uncertainty, quotation review, due diligence and pre-commitment decisions.

Evidence → decision01
02 / RECOVERY

Restore project control.

For stalled, drifting, disputed or inconsistently reported projects requiring a senior reset.

Exposure → control02
03 / DELIVERY

Carry the owner position.

For procurement, mobilisation, construction, operational interfaces, commissioning and handover.

Plan → delivery03
04 / CONTROL ROOM

Make the project visible.

For controlled decisions, risks, actions, milestones, evidence and executive reporting.

Information → visibility04
How appointments begin

Start with the decision—not the job title.

  1. Identify the capital, programme or delivery decision required.
  2. Review the evidence available and what remains unverified.
  3. Define the focused BAM scope and any specialist appointments required.
  4. Confirm deliverables, programme, commercial terms and information handling.
