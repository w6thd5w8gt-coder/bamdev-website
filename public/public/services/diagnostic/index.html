Purpose

A senior owner-side route built around the decision required—not a generic list of consultancy tasks.

Scope and deliverables are confirmed only after the available evidence and specialist interfaces are understood.

Typical scope
  • Project brief and decision-context review
  • Scope, quotation and procurement reconciliation
  • Programme logic and dependency review
  • Cost-to-complete and change-exposure review where information permits
  • Compliance, consultant and approval interface mapping
  • Prioritised decision and action register
BAM Development does not replace statutory designers, specialist consultants, legal advisers or regulated professionals where their appointment is required. The service identifies and coordinates those interfaces.
Operating route

Evidence first. Decision next.
