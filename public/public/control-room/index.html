Control model

One owner view. Five control layers.

Decisions

Owner, evidence, consequence and required date.

Risk

Exposure, mitigation, responsible party and escalation.

Programme

Milestones, dependencies, approvals and long-lead events.

Commercial

Commitment, change, remaining procurement and cost-to-complete.

Evidence

Documents, site records, approvals and controlled reporting.

The current public page is a demonstration of BAM Development’s intended control structure. Secure client login, data storage and project-specific dashboards are released only after technical, security and information-governance controls are in place.
