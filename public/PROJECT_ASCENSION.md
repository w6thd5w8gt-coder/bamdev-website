# Project Ascension — BAM Development Operating System

## Structure
BAM Development is the legal and master trading entity. BAM Maintenance operates as a division of BAM Development until a separate legal entity is commercially justified.

## 12-month objectives
1. Establish recurring fee income from development and project-control commissions.
2. Secure recurring maintenance turnover through managing agents, hotels, care providers, housing and prime contractors.
3. Complete public-sector supplier readiness and enter suitable DPS/framework routes.
4. Build Control Room into a repeatable reporting and client-portal product.
5. Create an evidence-led bid library, CRM and weekly management dashboard.

## Workstreams
- Revenue and pipeline
- Government and frameworks
- Maintenance operations
- Development consultancy
- Brand and website
- Control Room technology
- Governance, finance and compliance
- Founder operating rhythm

## Weekly operating rhythm
- Monday: pipeline, tenders and cash
- Tuesday: delivery and client decisions
- Wednesday: outreach and partnerships
- Thursday: systems, evidence and bid library
- Friday: finance, review and next-week reset

## 90-day outcomes
- Supplier profile and tender alerts live
- Public-sector capability pack complete
- Two framework/DPS applications submitted
- Twenty prime-contractor approaches completed
- Three paid maintenance trials targeted
- Control Room reporting prototype used on a live project
